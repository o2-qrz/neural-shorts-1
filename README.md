English Script for Robot Voiceover (9 Blocks)

1
Dry land scorched by the sun with no power grid and fuel more expensive than gold. Brothers waiting for help from banks and corporations is a dead end. They only want your money and subscriptions for expensive batteries. But we have a donkey hands and scrap metal.

2
Stop supporting bio slavery. A donkey is a living creature and it feels pain. If an urban engineer thinks it is normal to force an animal to walk in circles for hours at a well let him stand there himself for eight hours in forty degree heat. Walking in circles destroys joints.

3
There is an alternative. An animal should only walk in a straight line to the pasture to the market breathing the wind and seeing changing horizons. A local blacksmith builds a fifty kilogram trailing cart from old motorcycle frames. It rolls behind and automatically harvests free electricity.

4
Forget about fragile microchips they will burn in the dust. The blacksmith welds together two rear forks from old mopeds using original wheels and chains. With every step the hub motor sends forty eight Volts straight into the battery box. No complex clutches you can reverse safely.

5
Let’s calculate the gear ratios. A donkey at three kilometers per hour and a power of one hundred and twenty five Watts requires a twenty to one chain reduction. An ox or a camel provides up to five hundred Watts here we set it to fourteen to one. But a trotting horse at twenty kilometers per hour and three hundred Watts only needs a three to one acceleration.

6
The cart saves the animal in forty degree heat. When a camel struggles on a climb a hitch tension sensor detects the strain. The motor wheel immediately switches on to full power up to one thousand Watts and actively pushes the cart forward. The animal walks with ease.

7
An elephant at five kilometers per hour and a massive power of thirteen hundred and fifty Watts requires a thirteen to one reduction. To protect its neck during sudden starts a damper made from an old car tire absorbs the shock. Instead of software the smallholder smoothly flips four standard domestic circuit breakers on the go increasing the load imperceptibly. The driver's seventy five kilogram seat sits right over the axle acting as a perfect ballast against slipping in deep sand.

8
In just a four hour journey a three hundred Watt pump can lift tons of water from a twelve meter borehole a donkey yields sixteen hundred and fifty liters an ox gives six thousand and an elephant delivers a massive eighteen thousand liters of fresh water.

9
